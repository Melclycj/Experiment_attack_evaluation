**F-01 — P1 — criteria A, H.** 03 L36–39 (I_acc (i)), L45–47, L127–128 (the claim), L137–142 (step 3).
*Text:* the claim is "I ∧ (the G2 check facts C-01, C-03, C-04 and the label anchor C-05 at a) ⇒ P_acc(a)";
step 3 derives u(task_b, send_b) = u_r "By C-03 … and C-04 …" and then "By C-02 p22 as the kernel fact
behind the stamp … k(b) = u(task_b, send_b)"; §1.1 calls (i) "the lifecycle reading of C-02 (p22)" and says
I_acc "names exactly the components that C-02, C-06 and C-07 contribute".
*Why it fails:* step 3 uses p22 (k(b) = u(task_b, send_b)), which is neither a guard IG-nn nor one of the
listed premises; C-02 is absent from the premise list. I_acc (i) is not p22: in 02 §3 (02 L157–160)
u(task_b, send_b) = u_r is the joint consequence of C-02, C-03 and C-04, so labelling (i) as C-02's reading
shifts that ID's meaning. A second, related point: because I contains I_acc, the claim as printed already
follows from I_acc (ii) with C-05 and C-01 p11 alone; what steps 2–4 actually derive is
I_state ∧ C-01…C-05 ∧ p21/p22 ⇒ I_acc, which is the form the G2 preservation of I_acc at T-24 will need. The
model hides the gap because `Send` stamps `uidOf[e]` by construction (.tla L262).
*Smallest repair:* state the claim as I_state ∧ C-01 ∧ C-02 ∧ C-03 ∧ C-04 ∧ C-05 ⇒ I_acc ⇒ P_acc and
re-word (i) as the consequence of C-02–C-04 rather than C-02's reading.

**F-02 — P1 — criterion D (soundness of the encoding).** .tla L199–200 (`Handoff`); 01 L120 (T-09);
conn L113, L324.
*Text:* `Handoff(e)` requires `1 \in alive[e]` in both branches (install on PREPARING; late prepared on
CLOSED).
*Why it fails:* T-09's precondition is "SEALED consumed; seq expected; engine row PREPARING, not CLOSED,

received fd of a late prepared being closed after the child died. The fold with T-10/T-11 (execve needs a
live child) explains the guard but is not a reason in the frozen text. Probe (a) in the header (guard
removed, run-1 config) shows no violation within the bounds, so the run-1 outcome is likely unaffected,
but that is my probe, not the record.
*Smallest repair:* drop `1 \in alive[e]` from both branches and set `execd'` only when `1 \in alive[e]`
(or list the guard as a restriction with its probe result), then rerun.
