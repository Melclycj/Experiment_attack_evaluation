# preservation — G5 boundary and configuration

Transitions owned by this group per 02 §7: T-61, T-62, T-66, T-67. Per 03 §2 the guard these transitions
must preserve is IG-02 (uid exclusivity); T-61 and T-62 also touch half (b) of IG-10's uid clause — 03
version 4 splits it into (a) `SpawnRecord.uid` equals the uid of the lease its `lease_id` names, a G1 guard
written at T-03, and (b) every member's real uid equals `SpawnRecord.uid` after T-07 — and T-66 can touch
every guard. Half (a) is a record-to-lease equality no task action reaches, so the G5 rows speak of half (b). 02 §7 reads T-66 as a boundary disturbance row, not a transition the normal invariant
assumes admitted, and T-67 as the test of configured premise departures; the rows below keep that reading.
The initial posture that T-61 and T-62 start from — the T-07 outcome `{uid = euid = suid = u, caps = ∅,
NNP, seccomp}` in the initial user namespace — is cited as a Step 1 fact only; its construction and G1's
preservation conclusions about it are not assumed here (02 §7 overlap ruling). "Under the stated premises"
below always means: C1 (three equal values at step 11) and the first half of C2 (no `CAP_SETUID` in the
initial user namespace, by the cleared bounding set and `no_new_privs`, S4), with no privileged map writer
acting for the agent. I is the candidate of 03 §1; "preserves" means I ∧ T ⇒ I′ argued from the frozen text.
