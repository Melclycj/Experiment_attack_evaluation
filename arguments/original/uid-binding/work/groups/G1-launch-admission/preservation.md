# preservation — G1 launch and admission

Scope: the fourteen transitions 02 §7 assigns to G1 (T-01 … T-14), one row each, argued against the
invariant I of 03 (I_acc and the guards IG-01 … IG-13) from any state that satisfies I. Overlap rulings
applied as 02 §7 states them: the hello's row activation is argued here (T-12) while its bytes and check
are G2's (T-20 … T-25 are cited as Step 1 facts only); local withdrawal (T-14) is argued here even when
T-40 step 4 calls it; RuntimeCopy state is an input to T-03 and carries no identity (01 X-01). Source
abbreviations follow 01 (conn, rr, si, sbx, iv, mech, optb, kub, scope, tm). Evidence is cited by registry
ID; "[M] diag." marks a measurement taken under the 2026-09-11 diagnostic policy (04 §0). The TLC result
of 03 §5.4 is not inherited by any row: the rows for T-01 and T-03 argue the two reuse checks the subset
does not encode (03 §5.2), and every other row argues from the frozen text.

Reading rule for "from any I-state": the pre-state satisfies every guard — in particular IG-02 (a free
lease has no live task at its uid; a held or quarantined lease's tasks are its holder's), IG-03 … IG-05
(an empty fact is sound and names its event; a reaped PID 1 means an empty namespace) and IG-06 (one
lease, one holder). Rows use those pre-state guards; they never use another group's preservation
conclusion. Where a row says "T-nn fact" about a transition of another group it cites 01's outcome
column, nothing more.
