# preservation — G3 request and dispatch

Transitions assigned by the 02 §7 group cut: T-30, T-31, T-32, T-33, T-34, T-35, T-36, T-37, T-38, T-39
(ten). Overlap ruling applied as 02 §7 states it: try_dispatch's phase recheck (T-33) is a consumer decision
and lifecycle transitions may precede it; current callbacks (T-34) stay here; callbacks after release are
T-51 (G4) and their interacting route is R-GX-05, which is not argued in this folder.

**What I ranges over, and why every row below is a frame argument.** I = I_acc ∧ I_state (03 §1). I_acc is
stated per D-ACC event, that is per return of receive_step with a ValidatedMessage (01 D-ACC, T-24). I_state
is IG-01 … IG-13, whose variables are: the UidLease (state, holder, one release per lease), the Execution
row's state (open or CLOSED; DRAINING there is the row's state of T-47) and its epoch, Session.expected_uid,
the live members of P_X and their real uid, membership_empty with empty_basis, the SpawnRecord, the installed
Channel (its owned fd and generation) and the kernel queue on that fd, and engine_epoch. None of the ten G3
transitions produces a D-ACC event, and none writes any of those variables: their reads → writes columns in
01 §3.3 name only RequestEntry, TaskCall, TaskBinding, Grant and GrantState, the event queue and, on the
record-failure and stall paths, the engine's accepting_new flag — and accepting_new appears in no guard (03
§3 reads it only to describe the start state). The single state executor is the only writer of Session and
RequestEntry (conn L574); workers return facts and never write them (conn L574; si L111–112). So from any
state satisfying I, the post-state of a G3 transition differs only in consumer fields, and I holds. Where a
G3 outcome hands a trigger to close_session, the close itself is T-40 (G4): "calling T-40 from a group's
rejection outcome does not move T-40 into that group" (02 §7). Each such hand-off is named in its row, and
the state at the hand-off is the pre-transition I-state with consumer fields changed; what T-40 then does to
the row and the fd is G4's preservation question, not restated here.

Reading rule for the "conditions of I touched" column: "reads IG-nn variable" means the transition reads a
guard's variable (row state, dispatch_open, the label's expected_uid) without writing it; "none written" is
the frame fact the argument rests on. Evidence is by registry ID; EV-78 is the limit of every row: the
product receive path, the VerifiedContext hand-over and dispatch are stated E-02/E-03 integration
obligations, not built (iv L48, L88–91).

The T-30 and T-33 rows and justification row 5 were re-judged on 01 version 10 (the third freeze, after
the fourth design revision of 2026-10-18 removed grant_ref from every agent-facing message) and cite the
third-freeze blobs conn `0c16e567a34aa441` and rr `1a8ea18374b07431`; the other rows keep the line numbers
of their own run.
