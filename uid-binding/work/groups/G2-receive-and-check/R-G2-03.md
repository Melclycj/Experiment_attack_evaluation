# R-G2-03 — A causes missing/truncated or mismatched current ancillary to be paired with bytes and interpreted as a usable sender.

## 1 What the adversary does
A causes missing/truncated or mismatched current ancillary to be paired with bytes and interpreted as a usable sender.

## 2 How it would produce false attribution
- **Preconditions:** the adversary sends with ancillary the engine's control buffer cannot hold (many
  SCM_RIGHTS fds, forcing MSG_CTRUNC), sends before credential passing is in effect (kub §6.1 route A), sends
  duplicate SCM_CREDENTIALS, or relies on a receiver that pairs the data of one read with the ancillary of
  another. The unfilled credential renders as pid 0 and the overflow uid (kub §6.1); the route needs that
  rendering, or a neighbouring read's credential, to be taken as the sender of the bytes. Configuration:
  all three branches under the common abstraction (agent ↔ engine leg).
- **Transitions traversed:** T-20 (branch f with many fds, or branch a) → T-21 → T-22 → T-23 → T-24.
- **Sufficient condition violated:** C-01 p13 (data and control of the same read) or C-03 p32 (intact
  ancillary, correct ABI). B(R-G2-03) = (D_13 ∧ ¬q05 ∧ q06) ∨ (D_32 ∧ q12). Why it is a wrong attribution:
  bytes whose own credential was discarded or truncated would be labelled with whatever identity the
  receiver substitutes — a sentinel read as a principal, or another read's stamp — rather than their sender's.

## 3 What stops it
T-22's rejection of truncated, missing, duplicate or mis-sized ancillary over a fresh control buffer per
read, with SO_PASSCRED armed at T-04 before any sender exists, cuts the route at three points. Design text
(conn at the fourth-revision blob 0c16e567a34aa441): setup — the engine end has PASSCRED before clone
(wire definition L421–424) and the handoff requires SO_PASSCRED = 1, a missing preset failing the handoff
(E-01 control interface); pairing — E-02 step 1 (L453–455) re-initialises msghdr, iovec and control buffer
on every step and never reuses the last return's length, flags or credential; collection — step 3
(L459–461) closes every delivered SCM_RIGHTS fd, including the part delivered under truncation, rejects
other unexpected ancillary, and closes on MSG_TRUNC, MSG_CTRUNC, a missing or duplicate SCM_CREDENTIALS or a
wrong ucred length; step 4 (L462) requires pid > 0 and a non-sentinel uid before the equality.

Realization in the prototype (commit 3ec67985). Setup: the spawner creates the socketpair, sets
SO_PASSCRED on the engine's end at once and only then clones the child (EV-179, read at spawner.c:113–129
on 2026-10-19), and the trace of one sandbox-regression case shows the same order (EV-178); the engine's
install refuses a handed-over fd without PASSCRED, and the engine never sets the option itself (EV-177,
EV-180). Pairing: every data-channel recvmsg builds its own msghdr, iovec and control buffer on the stack
and the ancillary parse state is reset per call (EV-237). Collection: w2_ancillary walks only whole
in-buffer headers, collects every SCM_RIGHTS fd before judging, rejects truncation and out-of-band flags,
a credential count other than one, a wrong ucred length, pid ≤ 0 and the sentinel uids, and closes every
collected fd (EV-171, transport.c:90–122); the ancillary fuzz entry, the synthetic credential buffers of
the sandbox regression and a rights-carrying frame exercise these branches (EV-167, rerun after commit 7b4ab727 as EV-268 — w2_ancillary
is unchanged by that commit —, EV-176, EV-181).
Sentinel rejection on both paths: uid 0 and the overflow uid are rejected on the compare path (EV-175,
EV-176), and the recorder refuses a verified subject with a sentinel uid (EV-224, EV-225). Preservation
rows relied on: T-21, T-22, T-23.

## 5 Disposition
**supported under stated premises**
- Premises that must hold: the kernel at the pinned tag (AA-02); configuration: all three branches on the
  agent ↔ engine leg. Design-layer and kernel-semantics conclusion with the prototype's checked
  realization; nothing here is a claim about the deployed system.
- Evidence limit: integration run 20261019T011226Z-engine-pool-70000 and sandbox regression
  20261019T010923Z-sandbox-pool-70000 (2026-10-19), PASSCRED-order regression
  20261018T231806Z-sandbox-passcred-order and fuzz runs 20261018T231525Z-fuzz-transport-oracles
  (2026-10-18 / 19) and 20261019T025332Z-fuzz-no-c-parse (2026-10-19, after commit 7b4ab727). Shared limits: [coverage-uncertainties § Shared evidence boundary](../../convergence/coverage-uncertainties.md#shared-evidence-boundary-every-route-not-repeated-below).
  The checks' own limits: the PASSCRED-before-clone order is observed on one diagnostic-fixture case, not in
  an engine integration run, and otherwise read in the spawner; the six truncation and sentinel buffers are
  synthetic, not kernel-generated; a kernel-truncated rights message is covered only by the ancillary fuzz
  entry's constructed headers, whose structured mode leaves the last header unpadded and asserts only the
  flag oracle for a wrong-length or duplicate credential.
