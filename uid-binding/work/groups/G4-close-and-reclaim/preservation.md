# preservation — G4 close and reclaim

Scope: the fourteen transitions 02 §7 assigns to G4 (T-40 … T-52 and T-65), each argued against I of 03
(I_acc and the guards IG-01 … IG-13) from any I-state, using the frozen text and 01's transition facts.
Overlap ruling applied (02 §7): execution_empty even when it precedes hello, all control-sequence and
tombstone handling, the accepting_new latch on cleanup, the supervisor epoch input, and exit and queue
timing are argued here; no global atomicity is inferred (AA-01). Source abbreviations follow 01 (conn =
connections.md, rr = resources-routing.md, mech = mechanism.md, kub = kernel-uid-binding.md). Rulings
taken as given: a phase failure that keeps the original row's label is not false attribution, and
post-CLOSED processing is a preservation guard here (IG-13, former p72; 02 §4.1 item 3, §8); the
supervisor's domain-empty fact is assumed sound (MM-10, 03 §5.2) and its FALSE branch is R-GX-04's
subject. Every row's result is a design-layer or kernel-semantics conclusion about the frozen text, not a
claim about a built or deployed engine (the engine side of every row is `[S]`, EV-16).

## D4 — conditions that break uid ↔ execution 1:1

row's lifetime"; in 02 §0.3 the exclusive pool, C1–C4 and S1–S14 whose realization is not assumed) is
broken exactly where C-06 (p61 allocator exclusivity, p62 every task at u_r during J_r is in P_Xr) or
C-07 (p71 send_b ∈ J_r) fails. The conditions below are 02's branch cells D_61, D_62 and D_71 with their
ledger routes, plus the ledger rows that carry C-06 or C-07 as a secondary condition; each is sorted into
one of three labels with the E-04 design as the candidate under test — the reclamation double gate
(membership-empty AND row CLOSED; since 01 v9 T-45 also close step 2 done) and UidLease held / quarantined / free — against kub §2.6 and §6 as
kernel facts. Routes of other groups are cited by ID as the ledger lists them and not argued. The ruling
of 02 §4.1 item 3 and §8 is applied: a post-CLOSED acceptance with the original row's label is not a 1:1
break and is not sorted; it is the IG-13 guard argued in the T-40, T-47 and T-48 rows.
